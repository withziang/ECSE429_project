=== S1-06 create typical todo
$ curl -X POST http://localhost:4568/todos -H Content-Type: application/json -d {"title":"Buy groceries","doneStatus":false,"description":"milk, eggs, bread"}
HTTP/1.1 201 Created
Content-Type: application/json
Location: todos/3

{"id":"3","title":"Buy groceries","doneStatus":"false","description":"milk, eggs, bread"}
